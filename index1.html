    Where I Started

    I grew up and studied in West Bengal, India. I completed my Class X board examinations under WBBSE in 2018 (69.50%) and my Class XII examinations under WBCHSE in 2020 (65.40%). In 2023, I graduated with a B.A. Honours in Education from West Bengal State University (WBSU), achieving 70% (equivalent to an 8.50 CGPA as stated on my CV).

    Studying Education helped me understand how information is structured, explained, and communicated clearly. However, when I finished university, I knew that a degree alone does not automatically translate into workplace readiness. I needed practical, repeatable office skills that teams rely on every day.

    Learning Beyond the Classroom

    After graduating in 2023, I used the time available to me to focus on practical self-improvement rather than waiting passively. I worked on strengthening my command of Microsoft Excel and Word, organising digital records, and improving my written and spoken professional English.

    I also spent time studying how entry-level operations and business process services roles function in practice—reworking my CV to reflect real capabilities, practising structured interview responses, and learning how to communicate clearly with recruiters and hiring managers.

    What Practical Work Has Taught Me

    Since September 2025, I have been working as a Back Office Admin / Process Executive at Smart Capital, a mutual fund distributor associated with NJ Wealth. Working in a real operational environment has taught me lessons that only come from handling actual workflows:

    • Accuracy prevents downstream delays: Checking a client's documentation or KYC details carefully the first time saves hours of follow-up corrections later.
    • Consistency matters more than speed alone: Following standard operational procedures step by step ensures records stay reliable for the entire team.
    • Clear, calm communication builds trust: When answering routine customer queries over email or chat, people value straightforward, accurate updates over complicated jargon.

    What I'm Working Towards

    I am exploring opportunities in back-office operations, business process services (BPS), documentation verification, client onboarding, and customer support. My goal is to join an experienced operational team where I can contribute dependable daily execution, learn established enterprise workflows, and build a stable, long-term career.

    I created this website to document that process openly—so recruiters, hiring managers, and experienced professionals can see how I think, how I work, and what I am learning along the way.

    Workflow Approach

    How I Approach a Standard Verification Task

    An anonymised illustration of how I organize routine onboarding and document checks.

    1. Initial Intake & Checklist Review: When a new onboarding request arrives, I first verify that all required basic documents are attached before starting data entry.
    2. Detail Cross-Checking: I compare key fields (such as spelling of names, dates, and identification numbers) across submitted documents to ensure consistency for basic KYC support.
    3. Clear Follow-Up on Missing Items: If a document is unclear or incomplete, I note the exact missing requirement so the client can be informed clearly in a single email or chat message rather than multiple back-and-forth messages.
    4. Record Updating & Filing: Once verified, I update the internal tracking record accurately so the next stage of processing can proceed smoothly.
