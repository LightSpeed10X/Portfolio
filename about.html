About me

Still learning. Already doing the work.

My story isn't a perfect career ladder. It's a record of learning practical skills, getting experience, and working towards the kind of professional I want to become.

The longer version

Where I started

I'm Mostafizur Karim, a graduate in Education from West Bengal State University. I completed my degree in 2023. My education is an important part of my background, but it doesn't describe everything I've learned or the direction I'm taking now.

Learning beyond the classroom

After graduation, I focused on developing practical office skills and preparing for back-office and business process roles. That included learning more about data entry, document handling, spreadsheets, basic KYC checks, client onboarding, and communicating clearly through email and chat.

I've also been working on professional communication: improving my English, preparing for interviews, refining my CV, and learning how to approach recruiters and experienced professionals more thoughtfully.

What practical work has taught me

Since September 2025, I've worked as a Back Office Admin / Process Executive at Smart Capital, a mutual fund distribution business. My responsibilities include supporting backend operations, assisting with client onboarding and documentation, handling routine email and chat queries, and maintaining records.

That work has made the importance of accuracy and consistency very clear. A process is only useful when information is handled carefully, records are kept organised, and routine tasks are completed properly.

I don't claim to have everything figured out. I want my work, learning, and progress to speak more clearly than big claims about my potential.

What I'm working towards

I'm interested in back-office operations, business process services, documentation, client onboarding, and customer support roles. I want to contribute to a team, learn from people with experience, and build a stronger long-term professional foundation.

This website is my public notebook: a place to share useful lessons, reflect on what I'm learning, and make it easier for people to understand the person behind my CV.

Outside the job title

I'm curious about how people work, how communication changes outcomes, and how small improvements can make everyday tasks easier. I'm documenting the journey as it happens rather than waiting until everything looks finished.
