About me

Still learning, but already building useful experience.

My path is not a perfect career ladder. It is a practical journey of learning office systems, building process skills, and working towards the kind of professional role where careful work and clear communication matter.

The longer version

Where I started

I'm Mostafizur Karim, a graduate in Education from West Bengal State University. I completed my degree in 2023, and while my academic background is important, it does not fully explain the skills I have been building through work and daily practice.

Learning beyond the classroom

After graduation, I focused on practical office skills that are useful in back-office and business process environments. That included document handling, data entry, spreadsheet work, record maintenance, client onboarding support, and professional communication through email and chat.

I also began taking a more serious approach to professional development by improving my communication, strengthening my CV, and learning how to present my experience clearly and confidently to recruiters and hiring teams.

What practical work has taught me

Since September 2025, I have worked as a Back Office Admin / Process Executive at Smart Capital, where my responsibilities include backend support, documentation, client onboarding assistance, routine email and chat queries, and maintaining organised records.

That work has made one thing very clear: reliable processes depend on accuracy, consistency, and people who take responsibility for the details. In operations, a small error can create larger issues, so careful work matters.

I am not claiming to have everything figured out. I am building a solid professional foundation through real work, learning, and reflection.

What I'm working towards

I am interested in back-office operations, documentation, customer support, onboarding support, and business process roles where I can contribute to a team, learn from experienced people, and improve over time.

This website is my public record of that journey: it shows my work, my thinking, and the progress I'm making as I build a stronger career foundation.

Outside the job title

I’m curious about how work flows, how communication shapes outcomes, and how small improvements make daily tasks more reliable. I prefer documenting the process honestly while it is happening rather than waiting for a perfect version of my story.
